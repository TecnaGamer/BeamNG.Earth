# Credits: thunder grain bank v3

All thunder in this bank comes from field recordings released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0, public
domain dedication) on Freesound. CC0 needs no attribution; we credit the recordists anyway, with thanks.
Each recording was cut into short grains, its background (rain, wind, hiss) subtracted, and levelled;
the game layers and spaces them procedurally. Licences were checked on each sound page on the date shown.
