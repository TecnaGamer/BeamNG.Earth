Anonymous: reports carry no name, account, position or place. Each 5-minute window is added to daily totals and then discarded; a group (a GPU model, a CPU model, a preset, ...) is shown only when at least 5 installs are behind it, the rest are summed as "other". "1% low of a typical 5 minutes" is the median of every 5-minute window's own 1% low, so one slow machine does not set everyone's figure. What is collected and why: TELEMETRY.md.
