# European STOP face artwork

Original SVG drawings: `tools/sign_art/stop/`. The PNG faces are rasterized, cropped to the sign, padded with white at the edges and downsampled. Letter outlines and colours come from the individual drawings. Each image retains its listed license; the code and original 3D meshes use the repository license.
