- The RWB / RWBA 2000 pictograms from Wikimedia Commons (public domain; German traffic sign designs are official works, Paragraph 5(1) UrhG): ausfahrt.svg, bahnhof.svg, faehre.svg, flughafen.svg, gvz.svg, industriegebiet.svg, kreuz.svg, park_and_ride.svg, stadion.svg (sources in `tools/fonts/de_autobahn/SOURCE.md`).
