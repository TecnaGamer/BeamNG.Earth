# Town-sign lettering sources

D-DIN / D-DIN Condensed / D-DIN Expanded: Copyright (c) 2017 Datto Inc., design Charles Nix (Monotype). Unmodified fonts from https://github.com/amcchord/datto-d-din, released under SIL OFL 1.1. See OFL-1.1.txt. These are open DIN-inspired substitutes, not official digitizations of every national traffic alphabet. The French L1, Austrian TERN/Austria, Dutch ANWB-C, Polish Drogowskaz, Swiss ASTRA Frutiger, Italian and eastern-European standards are NOT claimed to be exactly reproduced by them.

AG Transport Heavy and AG EI-TP Heavy: drawing reconstructions by Andrew Garbutt / smugpie, https://github.com/smugpie/uk-ire-road-sign-fonts. See Transport-SOURCE.md. Contains public sector information derived from the UK Department for Transport working drawings; Crown copyright, licensed under the Open Government Licence. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/traffic-signs-working-drawings-tsrgd-2016-schedule-17 and https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/ . Irish forms derive from the Irish Traffic Signs Manual; supplied with the author's source description. The English and Irish lines are rendered only when their corresponding names are supplied; no invented translation.

Tratex Svart / Vit: unmodified TrueType files supplied by Transportstyrelsen / Vägverket, https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/globalassets/global/vag/vagmarken/teckensnitt/tratex_win.zip . The publisher states: “Tratex är fritt att hämta hem för illustrationer och trycksaker, men kvalitén kan inte garanteras.” Used for illustrations of road signs in the game and previews. Source page: https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/trafikregler-och-vagmarken/vagmarken/om-vagmarken/teckensnitt/ . No claim of an OFL license or government endorsement is made.

DejaVu Sans: Bitstream Vera license and DejaVu public-domain modifications; see ../DejaVu-LICENSE.txt and the existing regional_signs/FONT-LICENSE.txt. Regular weight is used for glyphs missing from the national-style fonts. The legacy alphabet retains the previous font metrics for unrelated directional signs.

The shared PNG atlas is rendered from these fonts. The font binaries themselves are not modified. Name matching, font selection and layout code are part of BeamNG.Earth; each third-party source retains its own license.

CCRIGE: digitization by Javi Casas / swaree, based on Spanish national road-sign alphabet drawings. https://github.com/swaree/CCRIGE . Copyright 2023 Javi Casas; 2022 Ministerio del Interior / Ministerio de Transportes, Movilidad y Agenda Urbana. SIL OFL 1.1; see CCRIGE-LICENSE. The digital file is a reconstruction, not supplied as an official ministry font binary.

Trafikk Regular: Aitor (2021), unmodified file from https://aitor.online/media/downloads/Trafikk-regular.ttf . https://aitor.online/work/trafikkfont/ explicitly permits bundling. See Trafikk-SOURCE.md. It follows the Norwegian road-sign specifications with deliberate modifications; it is not an exact official Trafikkalfabetet binary.

Modern Austrian/Slovak TERN and Swiss ASTRA Frutiger are not included. The Polish Drogowskaz digitization found during research has additional author-notification conditions; no such notification has been sent, and the file is not bundled. A generic repository licence is not used to override conflicting embedded font copyright notices.

Typoroute 2002 L1 100: Copyright (c) 2023 Gwenaël Jouvin, Reserved Font Name Typoroute. Unmodified TTF from https://framagit.org/gwenj/typoroute at commit d608a3c993f6692312b1b87ecb71d3a4cff1be84. SIL OFL 1.1; see Typoroute-LICENSE.md, Typoroute-README.md and Typoroute-SOURCE.md. This font digitizes the French regulatory L1 alphabet. Its built-in kerning and contextual internal hyphen are retained. It replaces the previous D-DIN Condensed choice for French town-entry faces and D-road cartouches. The author describes it as a test release with small geometric adjustments; it is not an official ministry font binary.
