# French village lantern carriageway clearance

`fr_lantern` was missing from `furniture.COLLISION_BOXES`. Unknown prop groups
are allowed by `RoadClearance.clear`, so replacing a stock lamp with the French
lantern bypassed the final road-clearance check. A lamp beside its own road could
therefore stand in a crossing street, even when that crossing was included in
the shared segment index.

Register the conservative bounds of the cylinder generated by
`tools/build_french_furniture.py:collision('lantern')`, using the 5.4 m catalogue
height. The existing check then rejects lantern posts intersecting any road or
deck traffic envelope, with the normal 0.15 m gap, scaling and height rules.
This covers both sidewalk runs and the no-sidewalk fallback. It uses full road
segments, including their interiors and neighbouring roads, not only endpoints.

Rejected lamps are omitted before `lamp_lenses`, so they do not leave an orphan
warm light or glow. Clear lanterns retain their model, spacing, country selection
and night lighting. No lateral relocation, new geometry, asset replacement or
regional-activation change is introduced.

`test_french_lantern_clearance` checks generated collision bounds, direct overlap
and clear pavements, both placement paths at a crossing, removal of orphan
lights/glows, source order, tile boundaries, scaling and grade separation.
The furniture, furniture-clearance, regional-furniture and rural-lighting suites
also pass. An in-game revisit of the reported location remains necessary;
BeamNG is not available in the validation environment.
