# A3 fixes on BeamNG.Earth 1.5.4-beta

Merged upstream `bd9c764cb0aa47ae65f1d4998d00c9517988796e`
(`1.5.4-beta`) into `codex/a3-road-streaming-fixes`, whose previous head was
`0df42a67b31b39f7d0f503b62bb39edf31d20f97`. Both histories are retained.

## Combined behavior

- Keep the A3 upper carriageway above its mapped underpass, final tunnel-cover
  checks, dirt-road exclusion and full-width structure clearance. Keep upstream
  bridge approach ramps, vertical profiles and bounded portal-cap depths.
- Use upstream terrain-triangle following for streamed road meshes. The local
  cross-width grid remains for the non-following fallback, avoiding a duplicate
  grid in the normal path.
- Keep FIFO incoming-message dispatch, terrain material preparation and governed
  collision rebuilding alongside upstream camera priorities, late bridge
  collision, directional importing and material prewarming. Preserve queue order
  through menu transitions and restore deferred prewarming after reloads.
- Combine portable process telemetry and safe capture cleanup with upstream
  delayed AI speed switching, route snapping and road-loading lead metrics.
  Conflicting speed options are rejected; unavailable process readings do not
  prevent summary output.
- Snap affine image coordinates only within `1e-7` pixels of an integer. This
  prevents floating-point roundoff shifting road paint by one pixel while keeping
  intentional subpixel placement.
