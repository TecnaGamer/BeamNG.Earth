# Real 3D road data (research, 2026-10-28)

The user: "Lets look at pure road map data ... tell me if OSM or overture includes precise elevation data for roads ...
Find a way to pull actual 3d road data".

## Verdict
- **Overture**: segment geometry is 2D ("Segment geometry is two-dimensional"); `level_rules` is z-order only
  ("not meant be a precise indication of elevation"); no elevation/grade field, no open issue about adding one.
- **OSM**: data model 2D; `layer` is order only; `ele` on 45,774 of 303.7M highway objects (0.015%), 1,625 of 7.38M
  bridges; `incline` mostly up/down; `maxheight` on ~425k highway objects (clearance under decks). No active proposal.
- Every OSM-based 3D project (osm2city, OSM2World, SUMO, CARLA) infers heights from a DEM + rules, as we do.

## Where real heights exist

Not usable: Sweden/Finland (draped on DEM), UK OS Highways (not open), US DOT LRS (M, not Z).

### Lidar (decks): measured
- USGS 3DEP EPT on AWS (s3://usgs-lidar-public, 2,283 datasets): corridor reads by octree depth: depth 10 ~16 MB/km2
  (1.1 pts/m2: 4-7 pts per m of road), ~1-2 MB per isolated bridge. Class 17 (bridge deck) genuine in LA 2023,
  Phoenix 2020; misused in Baltimore 2014 (99.8% of cells at ground) -> validate per dataset or use all non-noise
  points on the OSM bridge corridor. Class 11 (road) never populated.
- Copernicus GLO-30 at decks keeps only 6-21% of the deck height (LA Four Level, Phoenix Mini Stack): useless for decks.
- DSM rasters next to DTMs: Canada HRDEM `-dsm.tif` (same bucket), NZ LINZ dsm_1m, AHN, swissSURFACE3D, EA DSM.
- Texas Road Elevation Model (CC BY 4.0): stations every 3 m, robust mean of bridge/ground points in road polygons.

## Plan (tiers)
1. Ground roads: keep the DTM (already decimetre-good on lidar terrain).
2. Vector 3D sources (FR, NO, then CH, DK): per-road height-source override matched to the OSM way (bridge/tunnel
   flags must agree), applied as the source's Z minus that country's DEM, plus our DEM (datum offsets 0.5-2 m).
3. Point-cloud decks (US 3DEP, later FR LiDAR HD / CH / NL): stations every 3-5 m on bridge chains + approaches, height
   clusters per station, Viterbi/DP along the chain (grade, layer order, no-observation state), pins at ground ends.
4. DSM-raster decks (CA HRDEM DSM, NZ, NL AHN DSM, CH, EA): 20-30th percentile across the deck, same DP.
5. Worldwide fallback: rules + clearance data (NBI item 54B/10 for all US bridges; OSM maxheight on the road under;
   national defaults) instead of grading guesses for decks. Tunnels: interpolate between portals (no source has them
   except FR/NO/CH vectors).
First step: a probe tool comparing observed deck heights (3DEP) with the server's planned decks on bridgehealth.py's
US places; then lidarpts.py (Rust later) behind netfetch, its dataset index built weekly by the data repo.

## Implemented (2026-10-29, tiers 1-3 first pass)
Code: `server/roadz.py` (sources, per-cell cache `<cache>/roadz/v1/<source>/<z>/<x>/<y>.npz`, matching, datum),
`native/earthfast/src/roadz.rs` (VERSION 102: `las_decode` via laz-rs, `roadz_match`, `profile_clean`, `deck_lidar`,
`deck_blend`), hooks in `roads.BridgeProfiles._observed`, `roads.TunnelProfiles._observed_floor`, `grading.plan_roads`
(`obs`, coarse terrain only) and `grading.Grader._observed_lift` (an abutment is raised to the measured deck end, not by
the clearance rules). Off: `BNE_ROADZ=off` (all), `BNE_LIDAR_DECKS=off` (the US lidar). Probe: `tools/deckprobe3d.py
LAT LON R` (run from `server/`, with and without `BNE_ROADZ=off`). Tests: `server/tests/test_roadz.py`.
- Sources: IGN BD TOPO `troncon_de_route` (WFS, paged 1000; `position_par_rapport_au_sol`, Z -1000 = none, `fictif`
  links dropped), NVDB v4 `veglenkesekvenser/segmentert` (`srid=4326` WKT is lat lon z; `medium` L / U / B; NVDB flags
  few bridges, so map bridges may match any non-tunnel link there), USGS 3DEP EPT (index of 2,283 datasets shipped as
  `server/usgs3dep_index.json.gz`, 837 kB, `tools/build_3dep_index.py`; the newest dataset first, the next one when
  the first has no usable points or datum fit).
- 3DEP reads: the first octree depth whose nodes hold 0.5 points/m2 at a bridge (depth 9-10 in LA and Northern
  Virginia, ~0.3 MB a node), only the nodes the bridges' lines pass through (a box per 40 m of line: a curving ramp's
  own box took every node round its curve; 310 -> 119 requests at Springfield), 4 reads at once. All non-noise
  classes; per 4 m station the heights clustered into surfaces (0.4 m gaps), a Viterbi pass picks one surface or none
  (point share, grade cost, impossible beyond 10 % + 0.5 m, a "passed over" state), then spikes / grades cleaned.
  Datum: the dataset's ground points (class 2) against our DEM, median offset, feet found by the better-fitting scale.
- Check of the fit: in LA (class 17 genuine) the all-class deck agrees with a class-17-only fit to 0.01 m median per
  deck, 0.07 m p90 (64 decks, 897 stations). In Baltimore (USGS_LPC_MD_PA_SandySupp_2014, class 17 misused) the
  all-class fit still finds a 463 m viaduct 18.1 m over the ground (ours 18.0; mean difference 0.38 m), 9.3 MB, 6 s.
- Vector sources: Z relative to the source's own ground roads within 300 m of the part (median Z - our DEM), so a
  datum or survey offset makes no step at the abutments; ground roads take source Z only where `dem.coarse_at`
  (the DTM is better on lidar terrain).
- In BridgeProfiles each measured part is eased onto the graded road at its abutments and onto the mean measured
  height at joints (`deck_blend`, grade change <= 4 %); a part still steeper than 14 % keeps the solve; the result is
  never under both the solve and the measurement (an easing toward a map gap pulled a Springfield deck through I-95).
