# Recreation signs and road fixes

Brown supplemental exit boards use posted OSM park destinations and exit numbers. Recreation and forest entrance boards use mapped area crossings and verified guide placements. Textures and geometry are generated during normal sign streaming; cached OSM batches avoid per-frame requests.

Road fixes remove abandoned railway features, correct I-25 underpass grading near the Colorado border, and prevent rural ramp lights and erroneous utility lines on reviewed corridors.
