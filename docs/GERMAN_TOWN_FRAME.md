# German town sign correction

The entry plate uses a fixed 900 x 600 mm format (width x height). Lettering
is reduced uniformly or wrapped within the face; neither plate nor glyphs
are stretched. Other countries retain their existing layouts and fonts.

A separate rounded tubular metal frame surrounds the plate, leaving an open
gap. Four clamps connect the frame to the plate. The 26 mm tube, 25 mm clear
gap and clamp geometry are visual estimates from the supplied Hattingen photo,
not regulatory measurements. Road clearance includes the frame; the central
post reaches its upper edge. Existing metal materials are reused.

The German lettering remains the existing D-DIN substitute, not an exact
DIN-1451 reproduction. Administrative lines are only shown when provided;
this change does not add map data, infer districts or alter exit-sign content.
The optional 1260 x 840 mm plate is not selected automatically.

Source for available 600 x 900 and 840 x 1260 mm (height x width) formats:
https://mueller-vt.de/de/p/vz-310-ortstafel-vorderseite-600x900x2-mm-flach-ra2-c
