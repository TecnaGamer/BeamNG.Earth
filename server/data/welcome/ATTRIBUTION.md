# State welcome sign faces: attribution

Photos from Wikimedia Commons, chosen and aligned by wyo-backroads (PR #1118). Each face below is a derivative of the
photo named: the sign's face cropped out, its perspective rectified, scaled down and stored as a DDS texture. These
images keep their own licences (below); they are not covered by the project's MIT licence. The CC BY-SA faces are
shared under the same licence as their source photo. The depiction of a state's sign implies no endorsement by that
state.

States without a face here get a plain lettered board drawn by the server (travelboards.generic_welcome): their
artwork's reuse terms could not be verified, or the photo was unusable.
