# State welcome-sign assets

State welcome signs use real documented face designs, not a shared blue fallback.
Entries marked current_verified or newest_available are rendered under the user's
newest-available policy. Dated legacy references can be used without asserting
that their design or officeholder text remains current. The registry does not equate a free photo
license with an independently cleared license to every depicted emblem or artwork.

## Colorado

Current design confirmed by CDOT: https://www.codot.gov/programs/dmo/welcome-to-colorado-sign
Photo source and author/license are retained in registry.json (CC BY-SA 2.0).
The source image is unchanged. A projective UV mesh selects its sign panel and
preserves the wooden, cream-lettered design. The image remains under its source
license; do not relicense it under the program's MIT license.

## Wyoming

The 2024 scenic-postcard replacement is documented in WYDOT's annual report and
contemporary reporting. The older Forever West photo is not enabled as current.
The current face is included for local development with WYDOT attribution. Its
reuse terms have not been independently verified; it is not asserted to be public
domain or MIT. Review rights before public redistribution. Noncommercial use is
not itself a license. No generic blue or another state's face is substituted.
