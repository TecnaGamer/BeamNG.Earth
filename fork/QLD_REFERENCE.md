# Queensland reference sheet

Real-world look targets for the QLD fork. Compiled by Sonnet Worker from web sources. The official TMR/Austroads PDFs
could not be read directly, so many figures come from summaries. **[UNVERIFIED]** marks items not confirmed from a
primary source. Verify those against the TMR MUTCD / AS 1742 before building to them.

## Pavement markings
- **Colour**: white for all centre, lane and edge lines. Yellow is special-use only: a continuous yellow edge line = no
  stopping (applies to the verge/footpath behind it). Bus/T2/transit lanes are defined by signs, with or without markings.
- **Broken dividing (centre) line**: 100 mm wide, 3 m line / 9 m gap (AS 1742.2 via Austroads; TMR MRS45 item 52102).
- **Wide centre line**: two 100 mm lines up to 1 m apart, 3 m / 9 m (MRS45 52127).
- **Lane lines, multi-lane undivided**: 9 m line / 3 m gap [per Austroads summary].
- **Barrier lines**: solid white; double solid = no overtaking either way; solid+broken = only the broken side may cross.
  Historic QLD barrier width 80 mm [UNVERIFIED current].
- **Edge lines**: solid white 150 mm (QLD historic) or 200 mm (MRS45 52110).
- **Continuity lines** (through-lane edge at turn lanes/junctions): sources conflict on 600/600 mm vs 3/9 m;
  max width 150 mm [UNVERIFIED].
- **Stop line** solid, **give-way line** broken, both white; widths [UNVERIFIED].
- **Zebra crossing**: white bars. **Children's crossing**: part-time, red "Children Crossing" flags, 40 km/h school zone.
- **Raised pavement markers** (AS/NZS 1906.3): Brisbane uses yellow bi-directional, white/red uni-directional
  [UNVERIFIED pairing]; blue marks hydrants (informal).
- Sources: [qld.gov.au road lines](https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/safety/rules/road/lines),
  [Austroads AP-R578-18](https://austroads.gov.au/publications/asset-management/ap-r578-18),
  [TMR MRTS45](https://www.tmr.qld.gov.au/-/media/busind/techstdpubs/Specifications-and-drawings/Specifications/3-Roadworks-Drainage-Culverts-and-Geotechnical/MRTS45.pdf),
  [Brisbane BSD 3154](https://docs.brisbane.qld.gov.au/standard-drawings/Minor%20Amendment%20S/bsd-3154_c_raised-pavement-markers_standard-installation-for-traffic-lanes.pdf).

## Signs
- **Regulatory**: black on white with red annulus. **Speed (R4-1)**: white disc, red ring, black numerals, km/h.
  Size ~600–900 mm [UNVERIFIED]. **Give Way**: red (not US yellow).
- **Warning**: yellow diamonds. **Direction/guide**: green; **services** blue; **tourist** brown.
- **Font**: AS 1744:2015, derived from Highway Gothic (Series B–E width variants).
- **Route markers**: alphanumeric M/A/B: yellow text on a green rectangle with a white border; M1 Pacific Motorway.
  Legacy National Route shields white, State Routes blue, Tourist Drives brown [partly UNVERIFIED for QLD].
- **Freeway**: green boards, QLD exit series (GE2-3 Exit, GE6-9/10 End Freeway, GE9-15 Wrong Way). Galvanised posts on
  the left, gantries on motorways [UNVERIFIED].
- Sources: [Road signs in Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Road_signs_in_Australia),
  [QLD road routes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_road_routes_in_Queensland).

## Roadside furniture
- **Guide posts**: white; red reflector on the left, white on the right (two-way roads); ~150 m apart on straights,
  6–90 m on curves; culvert posts striped black/white at 45° (WA/NSW spec, QLD likely the same).
  [Main Roads WA](https://mainroads.wa.gov.au/technical-commercial/technical-library/road-traffic-engineering/roadside-items/design-of-guide-posts)
- **Barriers**: W-beam, wire rope (common motorway median, e.g. M1), concrete.
  [TMR barrier list](https://www.tmr.qld.gov.au/-/media/busind/businesswithus/Approved-products-and-suppliers/Traffic_engineering_and_road_safety/009RoadSafetyBarriersRegSupp.pdf?la=en)
- **Kerb & channel** (Brisbane): Type D layback, Type E barrier, precast mountable.
  [BSD 2001](https://docs.brisbane.qld.gov.au/standard-drawings/Minor%20Amendment%20S/bsd-2001_e_kerb-profiles.pdf)
- **Signals**: 300 mm lanterns (AS 2144, QLD MUTCD Pt 14); target boards optional, historically absent on mast arms.
  [MUTCD Pt 14](https://www.tmr.qld.gov.au/-/media/busind/techstdpubs/Traffic-management/Manual-of-Uniform-Traffic-Control-Devices/MUTCD-Pt-14-Traffic-signals.pdf?la=en)
- Overhead power on timber/concrete poles is the norm; street light shapes, Telstra pits, letterboxes [UNVERIFIED].

## Built environment (SE QLD)
- **Queenslander**: high-set timber on stumps (~2.8 m) with ant caps, battened under-floor, verandah (often
  wraparound), corrugated iron roof, external stair, whirlybirds.
  [Queenslander architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queenslander_(architecture))
- **Roofs**: Colorbond dominant. Popular: Monument, Surfmist, Shale Grey, Woodland Grey, Basalt, Windspray, Dune,
  Wallaby, Classic Cream, Night Sky (hex values TBD). Terracotta/concrete tile on brick homes.
- **House height**: max 9.5 m in most Brisbane residential precincts.
  [Brisbane City Council](https://brisbane.qld.gov.au/planning-building/planning-guidelines-tools/brisbane-city-plan-2014/fact-sheets/small-lot-houses)
- Post-war fibro/weatherboard, brick-veneer project homes, Colorbond/paling fences, pool fences, footpath awnings on
  shopping strips [UNVERIFIED details].

## Landscape
- **Street trees (Brisbane)**: jacaranda, poinciana, Moreton Bay fig, flame tree, brush box, broad-leaved paperbark,
  tuckeroo, hoop pine, silky oak.
  [Brisbane street trees](https://www.brisbane.qld.gov.au/clean-and-green/natural-environment-and-water/plants-trees-and-gardens/brisbanes-trees/street-trees/types-of-street-trees)
- **Bush**: QLD blue gum, narrow-leaved ironbark, spotted gum, Moreton Bay ash, she-oaks, paperbark, bottlebrush, wattle.
  [Flora of Brisbane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flora_of_Brisbane)
- **Soils**: Darling Downs black/grey/brown cracking clays (vertosols), red ferrosols on basalt, red-brown coastal soils.
  [qld.gov.au soils](https://www.qld.gov.au/environment/land/management/soil/soil-explained/types)
- Dry brown winter / green wet summer grass, cane fields, palms [UNVERIFIED appearance details].

## Left-hand traffic
Clockwise roundabouts (give way to vehicles already in the roundabout, signal left to exit), exits on the left, signs on the left, gantries on
motorways. Already supported in code.
