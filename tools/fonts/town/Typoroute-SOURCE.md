This is a digitization specifically of the French regulatory L1 alphabet, not
a generic DIN substitute or an official ministry-supplied font binary. The
author labels it a test release and documents small drawing compromises.
The original TTF remains unmodified. Its pair kerning and contextual internal
hyphen are exported into the shared atlas metadata. Optional SUR/SOUS ligatures
and alternate/tabular figures stay off, preserving the supplied locality name.
